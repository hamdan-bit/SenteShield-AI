    FIRST THINGS FIRST

    Keep your PIN yours.

    Never share a PIN, password or one-time code. Anyone asking for them is a warning sign, even if they claim to work for your provider.

    THE ESSENTIALS

    Four ways to stay a step ahead

    01Someone says your account is blocked+

    Do not pay a reinstatement fee or reveal your PIN. Open your provider’s official app or contact support through a number you independently verify.

    02You won money you never entered for+

    Unexpected prizes with an upfront payment or a short deadline are a common warning sign. Never send a fee to claim a surprise reward.

    03A link asks you to sign in immediately+

    A link can imitate a company website. Don’t open unexpected links; instead visit the provider through its official application or bookmarked site.

    04Someone pressures you to act fast+

    Urgent threats and countdowns are used to bypass your judgment. Stop and independently contact the real person or provider before paying.
