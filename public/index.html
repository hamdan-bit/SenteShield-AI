    GOOD TO KNOW

    Questions worth asking.

    Does a low-risk result mean the message is safe? +

    No. SenteShield reviews the text for warning signs. It cannot verify a sender's identity, links, or transaction details.

    Do you store my messages? +

    SenteShield does not save submitted messages in a database. Basic masking happens before text is sent to Gemini, but the masking is imperfect. Remove sensitive details first. Google's processing terms also apply when live AI is enabled.

    What should I do if I've already sent money? +

    Contact your mobile money provider through its official support channels immediately. Change affected credentials and report suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
